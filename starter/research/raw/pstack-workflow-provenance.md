# Workflow narrative and provenance

## Structure and claim boundaries

20 presentation slides, 18 minutes; one questions slide, two minutes; three optional backups. No shop or game example is used in the main talk. Plan → Implement → Review → Verify is the presenter's organizing model, not a verbatim pstack playbook or a claim that every task invokes four agents. Forking and customizing pstack is the presenter's recommendation.

The opening expresses a practical concern: working output does not guarantee maintainability. It does not make the unsupported universal claim that AI models were not trained on maintainable code. The guide author's observations about underspecified intent and insufficient context are attributed to her in the notes. No productivity multipliers, PR-volume claims, model superiority claims or cloud-platform recommendations are adopted as established facts.

## Article material

The user supplied the complete text of Lauren (@poteto), The Complete Guide to pstack Pt. 1 (August 31, 2026) and Pt. 2 (September 9, 2026). No original article URL was supplied, so none is invented.

The slides paraphrase these ideas; their Markdown excerpts come from the repository, not the social post's rendered code blocks.
